Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WebCraft Nigeria ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our website, book a call, or engage our services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, or email address, when booking a call or filling out a form.
  • Business information you share with us for the purpose of building your website, such as product details, pricing, images, and branding.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and general usage, collected automatically through standard web hosting analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and book consultation calls.
  • To build and deliver the website or service you've requested.
  • To send relevant updates about your project via WhatsApp or email.
  • To improve our own services and outreach.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with third-party services necessary to deliver your project, such as hosting providers (e.g. Netlify), domain registrars (e.g. Namecheap), payment processors (e.g. Paystack), or email/CRM tools used for project communication.

4. Data Storage

Information you provide is stored securely and used only for the purposes described in this policy. We take reasonable steps to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

5. WhatsApp Communication

If you contact us or book a call through WhatsApp, your phone number and messages are handled according to WhatsApp's own privacy practices, in addition to this policy.

6. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us directly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of our website or services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via WhatsApp or through our contact page.
