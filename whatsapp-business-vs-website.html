WhatsApp Business vs. a Real Website: Which Actually Grows Your Sales?

By Emmanuel · WebCraft Nigeria

This is probably the most common objection we hear: "My customers already order through WhatsApp, why do I need a website?" It's a fair question — and the honest answer is that they're not competitors. They work best together.

What WhatsApp Business does well

  • Fast, familiar, one-on-one communication customers already trust
  • Catalogue feature for showing a limited set of products
  • Direct checkout conversation, no new app to learn

Where WhatsApp alone starts to break down

Discovery. WhatsApp doesn't show up in Google search. If someone searches "hair vendor Lagos" or "bakery near me," your WhatsApp catalogue simply isn't there to be found.

Browsing at scale. Once you have more than a dozen or so products, scrolling through a WhatsApp catalogue or status updates becomes tedious. Customers give up before finding what they want.

Repeat questions. "How much is this?" "Do you have it in another size?" These same questions get asked over and over, eating into time you could spend on actual sales.

Trust for new customers. A stranger finding you for the first time is more cautious about sending money to a phone number than to a business with a real, professional-looking website.

The combination that actually works

The businesses growing fastest aren't choosing one over the other — they're using a website as the front door (for discovery, browsing, and building trust) with WhatsApp as the checkout (for the fast, familiar part customers already like). That's exactly how every WebCraft site is built: browse on the website, order through WhatsApp.

"WhatsApp is your checkout counter. A website is your storefront. You need both."

The bottom line

Your customers don't need to stop using WhatsApp. They just need somewhere to discover you, browse your full catalogue clearly, and feel confident before that WhatsApp conversation even starts.
