Who's Behind WebCraft

One developer. Real websites, hand-coded.

WebCraft Nigeria isn't a big agency with a sales team and a template library. It's one developer — me, Ogunremi Segun Emmanuel — building real, hand-coded websites for Nigerian businesses, one at a time.

I started this because I kept seeing the same problem: talented business owners — fabric sellers, hair vendors, bakers — doing real volume, but stuck running everything through WhatsApp and Instagram DMs. Answering the same "how much" questions fifty times a day. Losing sales because a customer got tired of waiting for a reply.

A website fixes that. Not a generic template — something built around how your business actually works.

"Your website is built for free — no payment required to get started."

That's the whole model. I build a real, working preview of your site first. You look at it, you decide if it's worth it. No upfront payment, it's just a conversation to understand your business. It's how I've built trust with every client on the portfolio page — real businesses, real results, no empty promises.

— Ogunremi Segun Emmanuel
