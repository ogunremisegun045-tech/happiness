Pricing & Payment

How much does the website cost?

Nothing to build — there's no fee to get your website made. I'll walk you through the full picture on a quick call.

Is there anything I pay for later?

Everything about how this works long-term is covered clearly on your call — no surprises, no fine print.

What's the catch?

No catch. It's a genuine conversation before anything is built, so you know exactly what to expect.

Do I own the website?

Yes — the design and build are yours. We'll go over how hosting and ongoing support work during your call.

Process & Timeline

How long does a build take?

Most previews are ready within a few days to a week, depending on how much content and how many products you have.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your product photos, prices, and any branding you already have (logo, colours). If you don't have these yet, that's fine — we can work with what you have.

Can I see examples of your work first?

Yes — check the portfolio page for real, live client websites.

Ownership & Technical

Is it a template, or fully custom?

Fully custom — every site is hand-coded, not built from a Bumpa or Shopify template.

Can customers order directly on the site?

Most sites are built with WhatsApp checkout — customers browse your catalogue, then order through WhatsApp, which is what most Nigerian shoppers are already comfortable with.

Will it work well on phones?

Yes — every site is built mobile-first, since that's where the majority of customers will be browsing from.
